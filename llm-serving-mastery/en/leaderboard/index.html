Submission contract

Register your submission repository. Create a private GitHub submission repository and invite the instructor account levshaazz in Settings > Collaborators. Register the repository URL in the course sheet. The invitation must be accepted and judge read access confirmed before the deadline. Registration does not replace pushing round-NN. Your submission.yaml author must match the repository owner's GitHub login. Never share tokens or credentials in the sheet or repository.

Push a git tag round-NN (round-01 … round-10) to your private repository before Thursday 23:59 MSK; the instructor account levshaazz must have accepted read access. Do not move the tag afterward. The judge begins capture at Friday 00:00 MSK and measures the recorded commit. Actual capture times are retained for audits; commit dates do not prove on-time submission. Public submission example and self-check. The instructor's private judge uses the published rules and thresholds above.

Open an Issue in your private submission repository and mention @levshaazz. Use it for access confirmation, individual startup diagnostics and appeals. Never publish your solution, private repository URL, logs containing secrets or access tokens in the public example. General questions. Students arrange their own CUDA GPU. Colab availability and GPU type are not guaranteed; instructor-machine development slots are not provided. CPU checks can be completed first but do not replace the GPU streaming smoke check.

FileRole
submission.yamlauthor, model (Hugging Face id), immutable model revision, stack — recorded for audit and printed on the board
prepare.shnetworked bootstrap only: install the locked environment and fetch the declared immutable model revision; it receives no judge seed or prompts
serve.shoffline measurement phase: start an OpenAI-compatible server on 0.0.0.0:8000 from the prepared runtime/cache, model name "submission", streaming /v1/chat/completions that honours max_tokens and ignore_eos and accepts requests of up to 4096 tokens
pyproject.toml + uv.lockpinned environment (uv lock after every dependency change) — the run must be reproducible
JOURNAL.mdone entry per round: what changed, why, what it measured — the raw material of your defense
Dockerfileoptional alternative — if present, the build must bake in dependencies and model artifacts; the judge runs it offline instead of prepare.sh + serve.sh

Model: Any open-weight model that can serve the judge's load in 16 GB of VRAM: 64 concurrent requests × (512 prompt + 256 answer tokens) ≈ 49 000 tokens of KV cache next to the weights. Your choice — and it may change between rounds.

Stack: Any stack and any kernels: vLLM, SGLang, llama.cpp, TensorRT-LLM, your own CUDA/Triton — as long as the contract holds.

Scoring a round

Among submissions that pass every gate, rank by S.
StepRule
DeadlineEvery round closes on Thursday at 23:59 Moscow time (MSK, UTC+3).
ContractThe judge snapshots your round-NN tag one minute after Thursday 23:59 MSK. The server must list model "submission" and stream a smoke answer within the shared 45-minute startup budget; a student startup failure scores 0, while a judge error is rerun.
Quality gateQuality gate: subsets of GSM8K, MMLU-Pro and IFEval through lm-eval against your endpoint. Each score must be ≥ 90% of the reference submission's score.
Latency gateLatency gate: p95 time-to-first-token among successful requests at 64 clients must not exceed the round's SLO (reference p95 × 1.25). Errors ≤ 1%. Every hidden length probe must produce at least 95% of the requested 256 tokens; an average cannot hide short answers.
Same modelLayered same-model check: hidden canaries are answered at rest and under load; the arithmetic mean of paired similarities must meet the published floor. Each hidden response must independently meet the per-request length floor, and diverse prompts must not collapse to one canned answer. Model metadata and pinned revision are recorded for audit.
SpeedSpeed score S = geometric mean of output tokens/s at 32 and at 64 concurrent clients (GuideLLM · natural-text prompts of about 512 tokens · up to 256 generated tokens · prompts drawn with a secret seed).
Mark 0–5mark = 5 × min(1, S / S_bar) if every gate passes, else 0. Each round is worth 4% of the course grade: points = mark × 0.8.
BarsThe quality gates, speed bar S_bar and latency SLO are published from a provenance-complete reference using the same judge version. Round 02 explicitly reuses the verified Round 01 reference and all original bars; it is not a new calibration. Legacy reconstructed bars are explicitly marked provisional and are not valid for grading.
OverdriveAmong submissions that cleared the bar (S ≥ S_bar): the top 10% get +2 Overdrive, the next 10% get +1 (rounded up; at least one student in each band; ties share).
Exam waiverOverdrive ≥ 10 after round 10 → the final exam is waived and credited at 40 / 50. The defense is still mandatory.
Dry runsEvery Tuesday the instructor runs contract, startup and smoke checks on registered default branches using the RTX 5070 Ti. The Start here page explains the schedule and result states. Reviewed individual diagnostics are delivered in your private repository's Course readiness Issue, not as public raw logs. No quality or speed grade is awarded by a dry run.

Policy: Cheating — zero tolerance. No late submissions: a round closes on Thursday at 23:59 MSK; a missing tag is a 0 for that round. Gaming the benchmark (caching eval answers, special-casing the prompt set, degrading output to win speed) disqualifies the round.
