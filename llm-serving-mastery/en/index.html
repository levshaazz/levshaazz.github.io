L12
The Thermostat

Advanced Kubernetes: single-pod concurrency & HPA

One pod already serves a crowd — continuous batching holds the GPU at 90 % with an empty queue, so utilization is the anti-signal, and queue depth, KV pressure and TTFT are the honest ones. The HPA is one formula — desired = ceil(replicas × metric / target), clamped — guarded by an inclusive ±10 % tolerance band and asymmetric windows (scale-up 0 s, scale-down 300 s taking the maximum). A new pod pays the 165 s boot, so scale-down is cautious; the failure drill counts what one lost pod costs. Declared arithmetic over the T6 working point: 250 tok/s per pod.
