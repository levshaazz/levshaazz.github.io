Submission dictionary

The words the leaderboard uses, in one place.

Round
A weekly submission window. Push the tag round-NN before the Thursday 23:59 MSK deadline; the judge captures the recorded commit at Friday 00:00 MSK.
Tag
A git tag in your private repository naming the submission (round-01 … round-10). Captured tags are frozen: moving one does not trigger a re-measurement.
Judge
The instructor account levshaazz with read access to your repository. Registration alone does not grant it: accept the invitation before the deadline.
Seed
The protected random seed the judge uses for hidden prompts and measurements. Students never obtain or submit it.
Gate
A published pass/fail check: quality floors, TTFT, canary similarity, error rate, output length. S is only ranked among submissions that pass every gate.
S
Measured output throughput in tokens per second at the judged concurrency, shown only when the required technical gates passed.
Snapshot
The one-time capture of your current round-NN tag. Actual capture times are retained for audit; commit dates do not prove on-time submission.
Overdrive
Bonus points for clearing a speed bar with margin. Overdrive ≥ 10 waives the final exam; it is withheld while final places are not assigned.
Participation credit
Round 01 rule: every confirmed solution in a registered repository receives mark 5 and 4 course points, without a tag or GPU run. No Overdrive is awarded.

Status legend

Published on the home card means the topic's material package (slides, lab, reading); partial on the schedule and Evidence planned on the topic page describe the same readiness in more detail.

Quantization scale Topic 03
Reconstruction step or scale factor. In an affine integer toy it has weight-value units per code; q = clip(round(w/s) + z), reconstructed w = s(q − z).
Zero point Topic 03
Integer code that represents zero in an affine quantizer. It is a representation parameter, not the floating-point scale or a model bias.
Nibble Topic 03
Four bits. Two INT4 codes can share one byte, but nibble order, signed encoding, padding and logical tensor shape must be specified.
NF4 Topic 03
NormalFloat4: sixteen nonuniform reconstruction levels, used with block scales in the lab. Four bits do not imply the same codebook, packing or kernel as signed INT4.
Paired discordance Topic 03
Prompt-level outcomes on which two candidates disagree. Equal aggregate success rates can hide different regressions and gains.
SSE data event Topic 04
Data dispatched after a blank line. One network read can contain many events or part of one; an event can contain several tokens or no text.
Finish reason Topic 04
Application field explaining why generation ended. It is distinct from the terminal DONE marker, connection EOF and validation of the answer's usefulness.
Incremental UTF-8 decoding Topic 04
Decoding that retains incomplete multibyte characters across byte reads, so splitting a character at a transport boundary does not corrupt its text.
Residual stream Topic 02
The hidden vector carried at each position. Attention and MLP branches add updates to it; the retained KV cache is separate state.
RMSNorm Topic 02
Per-position normalization using the root mean square over hidden features, epsilon and a learned feature scale. It does not mix sequence positions or subtract their mean.
RoPE Topic 02
Rotary positional embedding: position-dependent rotations of paired query/key coordinates. In this Qwen implementation values are not rotated.
SwiGLU Topic 02
A gated feed-forward branch: apply SiLU to the gate projection, multiply elementwise by a separate up projection, then project down to the residual width.
Logits Topic 02
Unnormalized vocabulary scores. Greedy decoding selects their largest entry; softmax probabilities are not a measure of factual confidence.
Admission control Topic 01
Decision to accept, queue, shed, or defer a request before it consumes scarce serving capacity.
Arithmetic intensity Topic 02
Operations performed per byte moved at the memory level being modeled.
Continuous batching Topic 01
Scheduler that can change the active request batch between decode iterations.
Decode Topic 01
Autoregressive phase that produces new tokens one iteration at a time using cached keys and values.
Failure rate Topic 00
Failed requests divided by all attempted requests under the declared workload and timeout.
GQA Topic 02
Grouped-query attention: multiple query heads share fewer key/value heads.
ITL Topic 00
Inter-token latency: time between two consecutive streamed output tokens for one request.
KV cache Topic 02
Per-request key/value state retained from earlier tokens to avoid recomputing attention history.
Little’s Law Topic 01
For a stable system, average concurrency equals arrival rate times average time in system: L=λW.
Operational intensity Topic 02
Measured operations per byte at a chosen boundary; used to place work on a roofline chart.
Quantization Topic 03
Mapping high-precision values into a lower-bit representation with a reconstruction rule, metadata and error.
AWQ Topic 03
Activation-aware weight quantization: uses activation statistics to protect salient channels through scaling.
GPTQ Topic 03
Post-training weight quantization that uses approximate second-order information to compensate quantization error.
Served model name Topic 04
The API-facing model identifier advertised by /v1/models and sent in the client's model field.
SSE Topic 04
Server-sent events: UTF-8 event framing over HTTP. The vLLM chat application adds JSON deltas and a DONE marker; these are not universal SSE protocol fields.
Prefill Topic 01
Parallel processing of prompt tokens that builds initial KV state and produces the first-token logits.
Roofline Topic 02
Upper-bound performance model combining compute and bandwidth ceilings; not a full latency predictor.
SLO Topic 01
Service-level objective: a measurable reliability or performance target over a defined population and window.
Tail latency Topic 00
A high percentile such as p95 or p99, always meaningful only with its population and failure semantics.
TPOT Topic 00
Time per output token after the first, usually decode duration divided by post-first-token gaps; not one individual ITL.
TTFT Topic 00
Time from the declared request start boundary until the first output token becomes observable.
CUDA event interval Topic 02
Elapsed time between device events on a stream; may include launch gaps and is not a sum of kernel durations.
Allocated vs reserved memory Topic 02
Allocated memory holds live tensors; reserved memory also includes blocks retained by the caching allocator. Neither is total device usage.
Kernel interval union Topic 02
Total time covered by at least one recorded physical kernel interval, counting overlaps once. It excludes other unrecorded activity.
Warmup Topic 02
Unreported execution of the actual shape/path before timed trials; not permission to discard slow measured repeats.
Clipping Topic 03
Mapping a value outside the representable range to an endpoint; distinguish it from rounding within the range.
PagedAttention Topic 05
vLLM's KV-cache allocator: each sequence's keys and values live in fixed-size blocks, like memory pages, reached through a block table instead of one contiguous buffer.
Block table Topic 05
The per-sequence map from logical token positions to physical KV blocks. Attention stays global across the pages; only allocation is paged.
Internal fragmentation Topic 05
Allocated-but-unused tail tokens of each sequence's last KV block. Smaller blocks waste fewer tokens but grow block-table metadata; the Topic 05 lab predicts this waste before measuring.
Saturation knee Topic 06
The concurrency beyond which added clients stop raising useful throughput while latency and errors keep growing; located by a predeclared gain rule, never by eyeballing a curve.
Closed-loop load Topic 06
A wave where a fixed set of clients waits for each response before sending again; the offered rate falls as latency grows, unlike open-loop arrivals.
Terminal-outcome journal Topic 06
The run log in which every attempt ends in exactly one recorded outcome — completed, rejected, timeout or error — and is audited before any curve is drawn.
Working point Topic 07
The one throughput/tail-latency pair chosen on the curve and defended against the SLO; not automatically the maximum-throughput point.
Pareto front Topic 07
The set of configurations no other configuration beats on both throughput and tail latency; tuning moves the front, the decision picks a point on it.
max_num_seqs Topic 07
The vLLM cap on sequences decoding in the same step; it bounds the running batch, not the waiting queue.
Backpressure Topic 08
Explicit, early rejection (HTTP 429) when the bounded queue is full, instead of accepting work that would time out invisibly.
Retry budget Topic 08
A bounded count of retries with backoff, sized so retries cannot multiply offered load beyond capacity exactly when the system is failing.
Disconnect cancellation Topic 08
The gateway duty to cancel generation when the client disconnects and to prove no orphaned decode keeps burning tokens.
Image layer Topic 09
An ordered, content-addressed filesystem diff; a changed instruction invalidates every layer after it in the Dockerfile.
Layer cache Topic 09
Build-cache reuse of unchanged layers; stable steps (base image, dependencies) belong before volatile ones (code) so small edits rebuild little.
Tensor parallelism Topic 10
Splitting each weight matrix across GPUs (column or row) so one layer's matmul runs cooperatively; an all-reduce per block stitches the partial results, so the win depends on model size per GPU.
All-reduce Topic 10
The collective operation after which every GPU holds the same summed tensor; in tensor parallelism it reconstructs the full output from per-GPU partial matmuls.
pod Topic 11
one running engine + its limits; in compose it was one container with mem_limit.
Deployment Topic 11
declaration: how many pod replicas to keep alive; the controller keeps them (in compose you restarted by hand).
replica Topic 11
one pod of a Deployment; the T09 fleet cut into identical instances.
Service Topic 11
a stable address for the fleet: clients hit one name, not changing pod IPs (lab08's gateway was exactly such a stable 127.0.0.1:8760).
requests / limits Topic 11
how much memory/GPU a pod asks for and may use at most; the limit is the same cgroup cap as T09's mem_limit.
QoS Guaranteed Topic 11
requests == limits: the lowest eviction priority; for weight-bearing pods it is the mandatory setting.
probe Topic 11
the controller's question «is this pod ready for traffic?»; for LLM pods — only after the weights load (T09 members).
rollout Topic 11
the pace of version changes: maxUnavailable — how many old pods may die at once; maxSurge — how many extra new pods may start.
Helm release Topic 11
chart templates + a values table; every update appends a revision to history; rollback = a NEW revision with old content.
GPU Operator Topic 11
a cluster operator that installs the driver container, container toolkit and device plugin so pods can request nvidia.com/gpu.
time-slicing vs MIG Topic 11
share one GPU as logical replicas (time-slicing, no memory isolation) or hardware slices (MIG, isolated, Ampere+).
HPA Topic 12
Horizontal Pod Autoscaler: a controller that adjusts replica count every tick from one formula.
signal vs metric Topic 12
GPU utilization misleads for LLMs (batching keeps it high); real signals: queue depth, KV-cache pressure, TTFT.
tolerance Topic 12
±10 % band around the target: if the ratio is inside, the HPA does nothing (no flapping on noise).
stabilization window Topic 12
scale-up is immediate (0 s); scale-down waits 300 s and takes the max recommendation seen in the window.
custom metrics Topic 12
LLM signals are not CPU: they reach the HPA via Prometheus Adapter, KEDA, or llm-d's WVA.
queue depth Topic 12
requests waiting per pod; the teaching target here is 2 per pod.
cold scale-up Topic 12
a fresh pod pays the weight load again (T09 members) — scale-up is instant on paper, slow in reality.
single-pod concurrency Topic 12
continuous batching (T05) serves many clients per pod — that is why per-pod capacity is a roof, not a fixed rate.
PodDisruptionBudget Topic 12
a floor for voluntary disruptions (drains, updates): how many pods must stay up — the T11 rollout lesson, generalized.
failure drill Topic 12
kill a pod on purpose and measure what clients see: how much capacity vanished and for how long.
PD disaggregation Topic 15
Splitting the fleet into prefill nodes and decode nodes; DistServe's answer to prefill/decode interference.
Θ (window throughput) Topic 15
Θ(P,D) = min(Σμ_P, Σμ_D): the split serves at its bottleneck's rate — a min, never a sum.
cached vs uncached Topic 15
KV-hit tokens are nearly free for prefill compute; billing them as full prefill work (t_c = t_uc) drifts the planner.
KV hit rate Topic 15
Σ cached tokens / Σ prompt tokens over the trace — how much prefill work the cache absorbs.
branch counter Topic 15
How many live agent branches still reference a KV block; pauses keep the counter above zero.
LRU eviction Topic 15
Evict the least recently used block — drops a paused branch's cache precisely because it has not been touched.
compaction Topic 15
The agent's own cleanup of old history: the KV stays resident but its reuse is dead — evicting it costs nothing.
hysteresis Topic 15
Switch the split only when the gain over window slices exceeds 2·SE — a burst is noise, not a regime.
switching cost Topic 15
Restart into a new role = the weight load again (165 s, T09 fleet); in-place flip takes seconds. Cautious direction: restart.
sub-agent fork Topic 15
A child agent reusing the parent's prefix: most of its prompt is already cached — that is the reuse LRU cannot see coming.
SLI Topic 13
the measured indicator: the number the journal actually records; the percentile and the window are part of the number
Error budget Topic 13
1 − SLO: the share of a window the promise may be missed; spending it faster than the window is the paging trigger
Golden signals Topic 13
the four per-request records (latency, traffic, errors, saturation) computed from one journal; a latency statement without percentile and window is a mood, not a number
Burn rate Topic 13
how fast the error budget is spent versus the window size; the pairing pages on fast+severe burns and tickets slow+mild ones
Alert fatigue Topic 13
too many pages train the on-call to ignore them; the opposite failure is the silent one — the burn-rate pairing resolves the trade
eval gate Topic 13
The deterministic deploy rule over a fixed eval set: promote iff the set is non-empty and every case passed - all-or-nothing; no evidence, no promotion.
shadow deployment Topic 13
Runs the candidate next to production without answering any user: quality is measured on mirrored traffic while the live path keeps serving.
canary release Topic 13
Serves a bounded share of live traffic through the candidate; the share is the blast radius, and the rollback budget comes from the observed error rate.
offline inference (batch) Topic 14
A second queue for work that tolerates waiting: bursts and jobs fill the slots interactive traffic leaves idle, and are priced separately from the interactive SLO.
cold start Topic 12
The paid delay between ordering replicas and serving: weight loading keeps new pods non-serving until the metric and the scaling window absorb the gap.
